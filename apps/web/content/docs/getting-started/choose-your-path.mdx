SINA serves a few different readers. Pick the lane that fits you — each is a short
path, not the whole manual.

## Frontend developer

You want a running component fast, then the details.

- [Quickstart](/docs/getting-started/quickstart) — install and gate your first intent
- [Primitives](/docs/primitives) — the accessible components you'll render
- [Use the primitives on their own](/docs/guides/primitives-without-governance) — if you only want the components, no gate

## Designer

You care about how it looks, adapts, and stays accessible.

- [Primitives](/docs/primitives) — the parts and their anatomy
- [Tokens](/docs/tokens) — color, type, spacing, and how they connect
- [Theming](/docs/theming) — rebrand safely, dark mode, and what's off-limits

## Product owner

You want to know what SINA is and whether it's worth adopting.

- [Why SINA](/docs/getting-started/why-sina) — the problem and how it's different
- [How SINA works](/docs/getting-started/how-sina-works) — the mechanism in plain terms
- [FAQ](/docs/reference/faq) — the quick answers

## Engineering manager

You want to know it's safe, auditable, and adoptable without a big rewrite.

- [Adopt SINA incrementally](/docs/guides/adopt-incrementally) — govern one thing first
- [Escalation & enforcement](/docs/concepts/escalation-and-enforcement) — what gets blocked and how hard
- [The audit trail](/docs/concepts/the-audit-trail) — the record every decision leaves
