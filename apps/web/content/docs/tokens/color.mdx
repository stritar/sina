You don't pick SINA colors by hue ("the green one"). You pick them by **role**
("the primary action color"). That's what keeps a UI consistent and re-brandable.
Click any swatch to copy its variable.

## Actions

The interactive colors, each with a **foreground** partner for the text on top.

<TokenSwatchGrid>

</TokenSwatchGrid>

<Callout variant="tip" title="The foreground pairing">
  Every surface role has a `-fg` partner — the color guaranteed to be readable on
  it. Use `--sina-color-primary` for a button's background and
  `--sina-color-primary-fg` for its label, and contrast takes care of itself.
</Callout>

<Callout variant="governance" title="Some colors are protected">
  Status colors like `danger`, plus the focus ring and the secure-surface tint,
  are **governance-locked** — a brand theme can't quiet them, because a security or
  accessibility signal must never disappear. See [the locked
  boundary](/docs/theming/brand-and-the-locked-boundary).
</Callout>

The full ramps (`--sina-color-brand--50…950`, etc.) and every value live in the
