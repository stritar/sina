The concepts pages explain *how* SINA governs. This section is the *what*: the
real rules that ship today, for fintech.

The Constitution splits into two kinds of rule, and every intent is one or the
other:

- **Governed** rules cover **actions** — moving money, opening accounts, issuing
  cards. They carry policy (limits, approvals, disclosures) and can escalate to a
  stricter component.
- **Display** rules cover **reads** — balances, transactions, holdings. They only
  check the *shape* of the data (and mask sensitive fields), then mount a
  presentational component. They never escalate.

## The catalog, by family

A representative slice — the [fintech reference](/docs/reference/fintech) has the
full list of intents.

| Family | Example intent | Kind | Mounts / escalates to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Money movement | `wire_transfer`, `ach_transfer`, `p2p_payment` | Governed | `SecureWireDialog` · `GovernedActionDialog` |
| Accounts & onboarding | `add_payee`, `kyc`, `change_limit` | Governed | `GovernedActionDialog` |
| Cards | `issue_card`, `card_control` | Governed | `GovernedActionDialog` |
| Investing | `place_trade`, `enable_margin` | Governed | `GovernedActionDialog` · `MandatoryDisclosure` |
| Disclosures | `disclosure`, `credit_request` | Governed | `MandatoryDisclosure` |
| Reads | `list_transactions`, `account_balance`, `portfolio_holdings` | Display | a presentational component |

<Callout variant="note" title="Many rules, three dialogs">
  Dozens of governed flows all escalate to just **three** real components —
  `SecureWireDialog`, `GovernedActionDialog`, and `MandatoryDisclosure`. The rules
  differ; the safe destinations are shared. See
  [Governed components](/docs/governance/governed-components).
</Callout>
